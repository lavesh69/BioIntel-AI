# Step217 external evidence boundary

Step217 adds executable software controls for qualified native-engine execution, model-package qualification, signed plugins and updates, enterprise backends, OIDC identity, interoperability connectors, evidence-grounded RAG and clearer information architecture.

The following outcomes remain external and are not created or inferred by this package:

- legal access to engine binaries, databases, model weights and hospital systems;
- target-machine installation and scientific qualification of each exact engine release;
- authentic biomedical model weights and independent cohort benchmarks;
- publisher certificates, trusted timestamping and SmartScreen reputation;
- deployed PostgreSQL, Redis, S3/MinIO, OIDC, FHIR, DICOMweb, REDCap and LIMS services;
- penetration testing, regulated electronic signatures, operating history and organizational approval;
- wet-lab confirmation, clinical validation, regulatory clearance or medical suitability.

All affected release gates fail closed until the required evidence is attached and independently reviewed.
