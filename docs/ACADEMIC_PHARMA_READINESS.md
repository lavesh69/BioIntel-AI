# Academic and Pharmaceutical R&D Readiness

## Academic research package

A complete academic package requires a research question, hypothesis, preregistration or protocol, ethics/consent statement, contributor roles, methods, parameters, statistical plan, data availability, software citation, verified reproducibility bundle, limitations and a field-appropriate reporting checklist.

A 10/10 implementation package improves transparency; it does not guarantee peer review, publication acceptance or independent replication.

## Pharmaceutical R&D package

The pharma profile requires:

- approved intended use and study protocol;
- randomization, blinding and experimental controls;
- assay ontology, replicate plan and acceptance criteria;
- sample chain of custody and compound identity controls;
- controlled records, verified audit chain and electronic signatures;
- leakage-resistant, independent and prospective validation;
- deviations, CAPA and change control;
- independent QA and scientific review;
- signed evidence manifest, limitations and release gate.

The framework is not GxP certification, regulatory approval, clinical validation or authorization for patient-care decisions.
