# Regulatory and GxP Readiness

BioIntel AI now contains a **validation-ready control framework** for electronic records, electronic signatures, quality events, controlled documents, audit trails, training and release evidence.

## Intended use and certification boundary

The platform must have an approved intended use before regulated deployment. The software does **not** claim FDA approval, regulatory certification, GxP certification, clinical validity or fitness for patient-care decisions. The certification boundary is the deployed system, its configured infrastructure, its users, procedures, training, data, validation evidence and quality oversight.

## Validation lifecycle

1. Approve intended use, users, decisions and prohibited uses.
2. Approve user requirements and a risk-based validation plan.
3. Trace requirements to design, code, verification and release evidence.
4. Execute installation, operational and performance qualification appropriate to the deployment.
5. Resolve deviations and CAPA before release.
6. Approve the validation summary and release authorization with electronic signatures.
7. Maintain validated state through change control, periodic review and access recertification.

## Data integrity and ALCOA+

Controlled records capture identity, timestamps, content hashes, versions and review status. The compliance ledger provides a computer-generated hash chain. These controls support records that are attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, accurate, complete, consistent, enduring and available (ALCOA+).

## Electronic signatures

A signature binds signer, meaning, reason, timestamp, exact record version and record SHA-256 digest. Production identity proofing, credential policy, signature manifestation and organizational procedures remain required.

## Quality system controls

- deviation, CAPA, change control, incident and complaint records;
- role-based training evidence;
- controlled SOP/document versions;
- retention, legal hold and disposition policy;
- backup, restore, continuity and disaster-recovery exercises;
- supplier qualification and periodic system review.

## Regulatory sources used for the implementation profile

- FDA Part 11 electronic records/electronic signatures scope and application;
- FDA electronic systems, electronic records and electronic signatures guidance for clinical investigations;
- EU/EMA GxP data-integrity and computerized-system expectations.
