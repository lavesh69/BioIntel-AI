# Regulatory Reference Register

This register is an applicability aid, not legal or regulatory advice. The sponsor must confirm current editions, jurisdiction, product classification, intended use, and binding requirements.

| Domain | Reference family | BioIntel control mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Electronic records/signatures | FDA 21 CFR Part 11 guidance and predicate-rule expectations | Audit chain, controlled records, object-bound signatures, access/training evidence |
| Clinical investigations | ICH E6(R3) Good Clinical Practice | Protocol approval, risk-proportionate quality management, site qualification, data integrity, endpoint review |
| Computerised systems | EMA guidance for computerised systems and electronic data in clinical trials | Validation lifecycle, configuration index, security, backup, audit trail, change control |
| AI lifecycle | FDA AI-enabled device lifecycle and predetermined change-control guidance | Intended use, model freeze, change plan, monitoring, revalidation triggers |
| India medical devices | CDSCO Medical Devices Rules, 2017 and applicable guidance | Applicability assessment, classification, quality/regulatory approvals, evidence dossier |
| Privacy/security | Applicable health-data, privacy, cybersecurity and localization rules | Data inventory, minimization, lawful basis, access, encryption, retention, incident response |
