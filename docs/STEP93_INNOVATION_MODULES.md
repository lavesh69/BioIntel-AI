# BioIntel AI Step 93 — Innovation Modules

Step 93 adds ten interactive scientific workbenches and a dedicated launch hub:

1. Pangenome & Variation Graph Studio
2. Long-Read Genome Assembly & T2T Studio
3. AI Protein Design & Mutation Engineering Lab
4. CRISPR Screen & Perturb-seq Analytics
5. Pathogen Outbreak Genomics Command Center
6. Synthetic Biology & Genetic Circuit Designer
7. Rare Disease Genome Interpretation Board
8. Biomedical Knowledge Graph & Evidence Copilot
9. Multi-Omics Biomarker Discovery Studio
10. Visual Workflow Builder & Pipeline Marketplace

Every workbench includes deterministic demo data, upload support where applicable, interactive visualisation, exportable tables or workflow assets, and explicit scientific-use boundaries. Specialist external executables remain managed by the Step 92 Advanced Bioinformatics Tools center.
