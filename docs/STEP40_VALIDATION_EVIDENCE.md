# Step 40 — DTI Validation and Wet-Lab Evidence Controls

Step 40 separates **implementation readiness** from **completed scientific evidence**.

## Analytical validation controls

- Immutable model, dataset, protocol and manifest SHA-256 values
- Preregistration, prediction locking and chronology enforcement
- Cold-drug, cold-target, cold-both, scaffold, protein-cluster and temporal splits
- Independent-source DTI validation
- Confidence intervals, calibration and uncertainty coverage
- Independent signed review
- Provider release identity, record counts, licence and retrieval metadata

## Wet-lab evidence controls

Wet-lab evidence is accepted only when raw outcome rows are linked to a registered laboratory/site, a signed site attestation, a versioned protocol and immutable artifacts. Independent replication requires organizations distinct from the sponsor. Multi-site qualification requires at least three independent organizations across at least two countries.

## Non-negotiable boundary

Templates, deterministic demo records, public-reference replays, placeholder hashes and self-attestation cannot be counted as completed independent wet-lab evidence. The platform can make the workflow 10/10-ready, but laboratories must produce the evidence.
