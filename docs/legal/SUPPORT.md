# BioIntel AI Support

Use the in-app **System Diagnostics** and **Store Readiness Center** before reporting an issue. Export the diagnostics bundle without including confidential scientific data or credentials.

A publishing organisation should add its active support URL, support email, response-time policy and legal entity details to the store listing before submission. These values are intentionally not fabricated in the source package.

For reproducible reports include:

- BioIntel AI version and platform
- the affected workflow
- the visible incident or request identifier
- steps to reproduce
- whether the input was demo/reference or user-provided
- a redacted diagnostics export
