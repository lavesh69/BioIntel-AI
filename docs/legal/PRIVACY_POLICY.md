# BioIntel AI Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 6, 2026  
**Product:** BioIntel AI scientific research workspace

BioIntel AI is a local-first biotechnology and bioinformatics application. This policy describes how the software handles information when it is operated locally or on a server controlled by the deploying organisation.

## Information handled

BioIntel AI may process scientific inputs that an operator deliberately supplies, including biological sequences, molecular identifiers, assay data, omics matrices, variants, abundance tables, structure files and research documents. It may also record limited operational diagnostics such as module name, execution time, error code, release version and security events. Interface preferences can be stored locally.

The software does not require advertising identifiers, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, camera, SMS, call logs or background tracking.

## How information is used

Information is used to execute the scientific workflow selected by the operator, provide requested reports, secure the deployment, diagnose failures and preserve reproducibility. BioIntel AI is intended for research and education. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice.

## External scientific providers

Uploaded files are not automatically sent to external services. When an operator explicitly invokes a connected feature, identifiers or search terms may be sent to the provider named in the interface, such as NCBI, UniProt, PubChem, RCSB, ChEMBL or OpenAI. The provider's own terms and privacy policy then apply. Administrators can disable outbound internet access and connected features.

## Storage and retention

The desktop edition stores data in the local application workspace. Hosted editions store data in infrastructure selected and controlled by the deploying organisation. Retention is controlled by the administrator and the configured retention policy. Authentication credentials are stored as one-way password hashes or managed secrets; plaintext passwords are not written to application databases.

## Sharing and sale

BioIntel AI does not sell personal information. The software does not include advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. Data is shared only when an operator explicitly uses an external provider, exports a report, or when the deploying organisation configures its own integrations.

## Security

The platform supports access control, rate limits, trusted-host rules, request-size limits, audit logs, checksum manifests, encrypted transport in production and role-aware API keys. No technical control can eliminate all risk; operators should use supported operating systems, strong credentials, backups and secure hosting.

## Operator controls

The Privacy & Security Center can export local preferences and text activity logs and clear those local files. Scientific datasets and regulated audit records are not silently deleted from the user interface because accidental deletion could compromise research integrity. Administrators can remove workspace data according to organisational retention and legal requirements.

## Children

BioIntel AI is a professional scientific tool and is not directed to children. Deploying organisations are responsible for account eligibility and supervision.

## Changes

Material policy changes should be published with a new effective date and release note.

## Contact

Support and privacy contact details are supplied by the organisation publishing or hosting the application. The in-app Support page and the store listing identify the active support channel for that deployment.
