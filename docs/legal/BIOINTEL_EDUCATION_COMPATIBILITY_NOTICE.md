# BioIntel Education Service Compatibility Notice

BioIntel Education Suite contains BioIntel-authored local workflows and provider-neutral service connectors. It does **not** bundle the upstream multi-user classroom, LMS, videoconferencing, search, notebook-server or offline-content platforms that an institution may choose to deploy.

The optional connectors are designed to interoperate with institution-operated services in the following capability classes:

- interactive-content and lesson services
- collaborative whiteboards and real-time notes
- browser and multi-user notebook environments
- randomized assessment and grading workers
- classroom Q&A and knowledge services
- learning-record stores and education search
- résumé/portfolio and credential systems
- video classrooms and virtual-classroom servers
- isolated code-execution and similarity-review workers
- offline learning-content distribution
- applicant-tracking and institutional LMS bridges

An institution may implement these endpoints with open-source or proprietary software of its choice. Upstream code, trademarks, licences, service terms, model assets, content licences and deployment security remain the responsibility of the institution and the selected upstream project. BioIntel branding in the normal product interface does not transfer ownership of upstream software.

Where upstream software is installed, its own licence files and notices are authoritative and must be preserved. This package does not remove or conceal legally required attribution.
