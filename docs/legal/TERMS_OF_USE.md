# BioIntel AI Terms of Use

**Effective date:** July 6, 2026

## Research-use scope

BioIntel AI is software for biotechnology, bioinformatics, machine-learning and research workflow support. Outputs are informational and research-oriented. They are not medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, clinical decisions, regulatory approvals, laboratory certification or a substitute for qualified professional review.

## Operator responsibility

Operators are responsible for the lawfulness, quality, consent status, licences and security of data they supply. Results must be independently validated before publication, clinical use, safety-critical use, intellectual-property decisions or commercial deployment.

## Scientific limitations

Bundled demonstration and reference datasets and models are labelled. Synthetic or reference benchmarks do not constitute prospective laboratory validation, independent clinical validation, peer review or regulatory acceptance. Probability scores and rankings are not claims of biological truth.

## Connected services

External providers are optional and invoked explicitly. Their availability, terms, quotas, data practices and outputs are outside BioIntel AI's control. Operators must comply with provider licences and attribution requirements.

## Prohibited use

Do not use the software to violate privacy, security, export-control, intellectual-property, research-ethics or biosafety rules. Do not misrepresent software-generated results as experimentally validated evidence.

## Availability and warranties

The software should be tested in the operator's environment. No guarantee is made that every workflow is suitable for every dataset or jurisdiction. Store distribution, hosting and support obligations belong to the publishing organisation.

## Updates

Updates may change features, dependencies, data schemas and supported platforms. Back up important work before upgrading and review release notes.
