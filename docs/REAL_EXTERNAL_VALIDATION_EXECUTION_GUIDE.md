# Real External Validation Execution Guide — Step 84

## Certification boundary

BioIntel AI can register, hash, review, score and export external-validation evidence. It cannot create clinical validity, laboratory accreditation, a peer-reviewed publication, GxP operating history, a successful inspection, regulatory authorization, patient-care approval or post-market history. Those facts must come from real independent organizations and official records.

Software test fixtures, synthetic datasets, screenshots without issuer traceability, self-authored certificates and unverified claims are excluded from the authentic score.

## Nine evidence domains

1. **Authentic independent clinical evidence** — at least two controlled reports from at least two independent organizations, with external identifiers and separate reviewers.
2. **Prospective clinical study completion** — approved preregistered protocol, ethics/regulatory determination, independent sites, model freeze, prediction locking before outcomes, predefined endpoint and independently reviewed results.
3. **Pharmaceutical laboratory validation** — at least two qualified independent laboratories, validated assays, replication and traceable raw data.
4. **Peer-reviewed validation publication** — final published article with DOI or PubMed identifier, peer-review status and limitations disclosed.
5. **GxP operating history** — at least twelve months of controlled operation under an applicable quality system, training, audits and effective CAPA.
6. **Regulatory inspection history** — official inspection evidence, authority reference, acceptable or closed outcome and no open critical findings.
7. **Regulatory authorization** — regulator-issued authorization/clearance number, active status, jurisdiction and intended-use match.
8. **Approved patient-care usage** — institutional approval, trained authorized users, human oversight, real deployment/use records and regulatory dependency.
9. **Post-market clinical evidence** — monitored real-use denominator, drift and safety surveillance, periodic review and closure of critical events.

## Controlled workflow

1. Create and approve a validation program in the Clinical & Pharma Validation Center.
2. Complete prospective execution and quality-system records.
3. Open the Real External Validation Center.
4. Upload the original controlled evidence artifact.
5. Record issuer, official identifier, jurisdiction, issue date and domain metadata.
6. Assign a reviewer different from the submitter.
7. Verify against an official registry, authority portal, publisher index, issuer confirmation, onsite audit or qualified third party.
8. Approve or reject the artifact with documented notes.
9. Review the nine-domain scorecard and remediate every blocker.
10. Mark the validation program completed only after sponsor quality authorization.
11. Export the checksum-bound validation dossier.

## Claim gate

A real validated-use claim is blocked unless all nine domains score 10/10 and the controlled validation program is completed. Even then, the permitted use remains limited to the documented intended use, jurisdiction, institution and authorization conditions.
