# Step 89 — Independent Validation Accelerator Guide

The accelerator converts the real external-validation scorecard into an operational, owner-assigned plan and a
checksum-bound partner pack. It does not replace external execution and never edits the authentic score.

A genuine 10/10 program requires, for the documented intended use, authentic evidence across all nine domains:
independent clinical reports from at least two independent organizations; a preregistered prospective multisite
study with locked predictions and predefined endpoint success; independent pharmaceutical laboratory replication;
a peer-reviewed validation publication; completed GxP operating history; regulatory inspection history; applicable
regulatory authorization; approved patient-care deployment; and post-market clinical evidence.

## Workflow

1. Create and approve the validation program and intended-use statement.
2. Open the Independent Validation Accelerator and export the partner execution pack.
3. Qualify independent sites and assign sponsor, investigator, adjudicator, statistician and quality roles.
4. Approve/preregister the protocol and statistical analysis plan before outcomes are visible.
5. Freeze the model and lock blinded predictions.
6. Import independently adjudicated outcomes and execute the predefined analysis.
7. Register original issuer artifacts with stable identifiers and checksums.
8. Complete review by a person different from the submitter.
9. Close deviations/CAPA and sign the validation dossier.
10. Re-evaluate the authentic score. Only genuine accepted evidence can reach 10/10.

The pathway should be interpreted against the applicable jurisdiction, product classification and intended use.
