# BioIntel Classroom User Guide

Open **BioIntel Classroom** from the Education category. Enter your existing BioIntel user ID and select/create an institution. Students can see their role-aware dashboard, enrolled subjects, attendance, pending assignments, published results and authorized circulars. Records are local-first and durable under `BIOINTEL_USER_DATA_DIR\education`; no sample marks or attendance are generated. Ask an institution administrator if access is denied—cross-student and cross-institution access is blocked by design.
