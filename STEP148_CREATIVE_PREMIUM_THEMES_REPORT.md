# Step 148 — Six Creative Premium Themes

BioIntel AI Step 147 was used as the cumulative base. This release adds six complete full-interface themes while preserving all existing scientific modules, Research Lifecycle workspaces, fonts, accessibility controls and Step 145 stability protections.

## New themes

| Theme | Main direction | Experience | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solar Helix | Yellow / gold / cyan | Solar kinetic helix | Bold dashboards and presentations |
| Black Diamond | True black / platinum / ice blue | Faceted black diamond | Low-light analysis and executive review |
| Crimson Genome | Red / crimson / warm gold | Crimson genomic pulse | Genomics, safety and critical review |
| Electric Lime | Lime / chartreuse / cyan | Electric lime circuit | Bioinformatics and live technical workflows |
| Velvet Neon | Magenta / violet / cyan | Velvet neon scientific lounge | AI discovery and premium presentations |
| Copper Eclipse | Copper / amber / turquoise | Copper eclipse scientific atelier | Structural biology and scientific storytelling |

## What changes per theme

The six themes are not colour-only skins. Each includes:

- Dedicated dark palette
- Dedicated light palette
- Unique page background and scientific motif
- Unique sidebar and navigation treatment
- Unique hero geometry and materials
- Unique card, KPI and table surfaces
- Unique control and chip geometry
- Unique buttons and active navigation states
- Unique chart line, marker and grid profile
- Dedicated motion personality
- Theme search metadata and recommended fonts
- Appearance Studio preview, comparison and health scoring
