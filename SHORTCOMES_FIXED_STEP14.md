# Step 14 — Professional Shortcomings Fixed

## No milestone governance

Projects had status and target dates but no controlled delivery milestones.
Step 14 adds due dates, owners, acceptance criteria, evidence references and
blocked/completed states.

## No decision rationale

Important model and data choices could be lost in notes. Step 14 adds an
append-only decision log with context, rationale, alternatives and impact.

## No release approval controls

Projects lacked explicit approval gates. Step 14 adds standard and custom gates
with reviewer labels, conditions, evidence and decision dates.

## Weak reproducibility handoff

Workspace reports were informative but not a controlled project package.
Step 14 creates a versioned ZIP with environment metadata and per-file SHA-256.

## No package verification

Portable files could not be independently checked. Evidence Package Studio
validates checksums, path safety, format version and manifest consistency.

## Audit log lacked tamper evidence

Step 14 hash-chains new privacy-safe audit events and verifies the chain in
diagnostics, API, CLI and the release-readiness screen.

## Project deletion left governance risk

Project deletion now cascades to milestones, decisions and gates while leaving
datasets and model artifacts untouched.

## Project import could partially fail

Project bundles are fully prevalidated before project creation, reducing the
risk of partially imported governance records.

## No formal readiness model

Step 14 scores project definition, evidence linkage, governance controls,
diagnostics, audit integrity and experiments on a transparent 100-point matrix.
