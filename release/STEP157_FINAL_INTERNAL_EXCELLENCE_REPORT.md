## Completed internal improvements

Twelve protected specialist plans were added for FastQC, MultiQC, Fastp, Cutadapt, SnpEff, InterProScan, HMMER, BLASTp, Mosdepth, Picard, Salmon and Kallisto. ### Full-file streaming integrity

The new CSV/TSV streaming profiler provides:

- complete input-file SHA-256 hashing;
- chunked bounded-memory statistics;
- atomic checkpoint persistence;
- safe resume after interruption;
- schema-drift detection;
- missingness and data-type summaries;
- online numeric mean, variance, range and bounded-cardinality summaries;
- a downloadable reproducibility bundle.

This complements the interactive, extended and full native-analysis modes, which support up to 250,000, 750,000 and 1,000,000 rows respectively, with up to 512 selected numeric features in full mode.
