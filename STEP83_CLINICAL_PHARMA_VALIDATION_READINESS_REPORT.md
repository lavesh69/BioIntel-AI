# Step 83 Clinical & Pharmaceutical Validation Readiness Report

## Outcome

BioIntel AI is now prepared as a **validation-execution-ready research platform**. The previous checklist-level readiness layer has been upgraded into an executable registry and release-gating system.

It remains **not clinically/pharmaceutically validated** until authentic external execution is completed for a defined intended use and frozen configuration.

## Claim profiles

### Research/demo

Research-only by design. No clinical or validated-pharmaceutical claim can be released.

### Pharmaceutical R&D decision support

Requires at least two qualified independent sites, two prospective outcome batches, approved primary and per-site results, complete required evidence categories, training, CAPA effectiveness, valid audit chain, signed validation summary and completed status.

### Clinical decision-support candidate

Adds at least three independent sites/outcome batches, ethics/regulatory and clinical-evaluation evidence, privacy/security, human factors, post-deployment surveillance and approved predefined subgroup analysis. Regulatory authorization remains jurisdiction-specific even after the software gate passes.

## 23 release gates

1. Intended use
2. Prohibited uses/claim boundary
3. Approved protocol
4. Predefined endpoint, criterion, sample size and statistics
5. Ethics/regulatory determination
6. Prospective preregistration
7. Qualified independent sites
8. Immutable model/input freeze
9. Predictions locked before outcomes
10. Blinding and adjudication
11. Outcome-batch coverage
12. Sample-size reconciliation
13. Approved primary endpoint result
14. Per-site performance
15. Predefined subgroup analysis where applicable
16. Required evidence categories
17. Independent evidence provenance
18. No open major/critical events
19. CAPA effectiveness
20. Role-based training
21. Tamper-evident audit chain
22. Signed validation summary
23. Completed quality-release status

## External work still required

- sponsor-approved intended use and regulatory applicability assessment
- authentic licensed datasets and assay/clinical outcomes
- independent qualified laboratories/sites and personnel
- approved/preregistered prospective protocol
- blinded prediction execution before outcome access
- independently adjudicated outcomes and statistical review
- quality-system execution, deviations/CAPA, training and approvals
- jurisdiction-specific submissions/authorizations where applicable
- sustained post-deployment monitoring and operating history

## Release rule

Any failed critical gate forces the permitted claim to remain: **“Research/demo use only; clinical and validated pharmaceutical claims remain blocked.”**
