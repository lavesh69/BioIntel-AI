# BioIntel Connect moderation model

Report taxonomy covers spam, harassment, impersonation, fake credentials, plagiarism, copyright, scientific misinformation, fabricated research, privacy/patient exposure, dangerous laboratory content and undisclosed promotion.

Workflow:

1. Permission-checked report submission preserves evidence and creates a moderation case.
2. Moderator/admin reviews the target, reason and detail.
3. A reasoned action is selected: no action, warning, hidden/removed content, account restriction/suspension/disablement.
4. The case and immutable administrative action are stored and audited.
5. The affected user may appeal with evidence.
6. A reviewer records upheld, overturned or modified resolution.

Verification rejection has a separate appeal-to-pending flow. Community hosts/moderators may hide live comments, mute or remove participants. Administrative dashboards and action routes are role-gated. Legal holds require administrator/local-operator privilege. Tests verify RBAC denial and role-authorized decisions/operations.
