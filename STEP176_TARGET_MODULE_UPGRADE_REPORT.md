## Rating boundary

The internal upgrade layer can be verified at 10/10. Actual clinical, public-health, translational or controlled-release readiness remains evidence-bound and requires the real engines, licensed/official reference releases, independent cohorts/sites, wet-lab or orthogonal confirmation, operating history, expert sign-off and formal release review.
