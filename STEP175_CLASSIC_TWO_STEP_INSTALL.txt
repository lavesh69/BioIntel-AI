BioIntel AI Step 175 - Classic Windows Method

1. Extract all four matching Step 175 ZIP parts into one new empty folder.
2. Open the extracted BioIntel-AI folder.
3. Double-click INSTALL_WINDOWS.bat and wait for INSTALLATION COMPLETE.
4. Close the installer window.
5. Double-click RUN_BOTH_WINDOWS.bat whenever you want to start BioIntel AI.

Do not mix V2, V3, V4, original, fixed, or hotfix ZIP parts in the same folder.
