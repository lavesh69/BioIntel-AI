# BioIntel Connect complete-target blueprint

Release target: BioIntel AI 15.6.0 scientific community and social-network upgrade, including master-prompt phases 1–7 and the final feature-completeness addendum.

## Delivered capabilities

- Professional profiles, follows, professional connections, blocking, muting and privacy defaults.
- Secure posts, multi-media uploads, feeds, comments/replies, scientific reactions, reposts, bookmarks, mentions, notifications and moderation reports.
- Durable direct/group messaging, requests, delivery/read receipts, revision history, reactions, saved messages, typing events and offline realtime replay.
- Moderated scientific communities with public/private/invite/institution/verified visibility, channels, join approval and role-based posting.
- Collaboration requests/applications, research projects, publications with authorship confirmation, dataset access, workflows/versions/forks, events and opportunities.
- Permission-filtered search, explainable recommendations/feedback, and Shiro approval-gated drafting that never sends or publishes.
- Stories/highlights, accessible short videos, live streams and consent-aware audio/video/screen-sharing calls.
- Email/professional verification, resumable 12-step onboarding, drafts, autosave revisions, scheduling/retry, notification delivery/digests and lifecycle history.
- Role-gated administration, moderation appeals/actions, legal holds, encrypted backups, isolated restore validation, health/capacity alerts and safe cleanup previews.
- English, Hindi and Hinglish preferences; reduced-motion, high-contrast, data-saving and caption controls; audio autoplay remains disabled.

## Non-negotiable safety properties

Visibility is enforced in services and queries, not merely hidden in UI. Blocking is symmetric for relationship/content/message access. Scientific workflows may be forked but are never auto-executed. Shiro generates reviewable drafts only. Recording requires explicit participant consent. Backup success is not claimed until checksum, decryption, ZIP CRC, SQLite integrity and migration checks pass in an isolated restore directory.
