# Phase 33 Digital Twin Safety and Limitations

- Every output is marked research/education simulation, non-clinical, not medical advice, not a prescription, and not a guaranteed outcome.
- Synthetic/reference data is the default and is never silently mixed with real records.
- Direct identity fields are rejected; the software does not infer identity.
- Patient-derived research requires governed consent metadata and explicit acknowledgement.
- Ethics approval is never inferred from a reference string.
- Built-in models are compact learning models with synthetic parameters, not validated mechanistic or physiological twins.
- Imported models are stored but cannot execute without an approved adapter.
- Uncertainty bands represent model-assumption ranges, not clinical confidence intervals.
- Clinical Validation and Clinical-Grade Twin are unavailable in this release.
- Institutional privacy, ethics, retention, access, model review, and external scientific validation remain required for real research use.
