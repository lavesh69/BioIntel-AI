# BioIntel AI: A Modular Windows Platform for Reproducible Bioinformatics and Biomedical AI Workflows

## Abstract
BioIntel AI integrates 80 major bioinformatics, biomedical AI and platform modules within a Windows-first workspace. In the Step 191 internal reproducibility benchmark, 80/80 deterministic module workflows completed three repeat runs with matching output hashes; mean F1 was 1.000 and mean per-module runtime was 0.0043 seconds on the build environment. These results measure internal software execution on synthetic fixtures and do not establish clinical validity or superiority to independent tools.
