# Phase 33 Biomedical Digital Twin Architecture

The Biomedical Digital Twin Learning Lab is a persistent research/education simulation workspace, not a clinically validated human twin.

Layers:

- Versioned project, collaborator, consent, provenance, retention, and audit records.
- Validated observation ingestion with units, ranges, missingness, quality, de-identification, consent, duplicate SHA-256, and explicit synthetic flags.
- Deterministic synthetic profile generator with seed, version, assumptions, distributions, constraints, and non-clinical label.
- Model registry separating executable built-in learning models from stored imported definitions.
- Scenario engine with baseline, interventions, temporal steps, replay, uncertainty bands, parameter sensitivity, and comparison.
- Validation records that require reviewer evidence and can never auto-promote to Clinical Validation.
- Review-required links to Shiro, Science Pulse, Virtual Lab, Classroom, Academy, Examination, Intelligence Hub, Research Compute, Studio, Portfolio, Research Projects, and Scientific Agents.
- FastAPI, Streamlit, and watermarked reproducibility bundles.

Clinical-Grade Twin is disabled. Patient-derived research requires consent governance and explicit non-clinical acknowledgement before simulation.
