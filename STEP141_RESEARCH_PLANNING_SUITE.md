# Step 141 Research Planning Suite

Step 141 expands the Research Planning category from two workspaces to eighteen by adding sixteen specialist planning modules. The existing Biostatistics and Synthetic Cohort modules remain unchanged and connected through handoffs.

Each new module provides:

- Guided and Expert modes
- CSV, TSV, JSON and Excel input with schema mapping
- A bounded starter dataset and downloadable template
- Explicit run-on-demand analysis
- Eight module-specific interactive visuals
- Scientific interpretation, confidence, limitations and next action
- Persistent record review and version states
- Cross-module handoff context
- SHA-256-bound reproducibility ZIP exports
- Safe error isolation and stable recovery identifiers

The suite is intended for research planning. It does not replace ethics, regulatory, clinical, statistical, safety or organisational approval.
