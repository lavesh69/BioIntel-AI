# BioIntel Education Phase 11 — App Experience Report

## New Mega Production & Academic Center

The Phase 11 desktop application consolidates production and academic governance into six workspaces:

1. Overview — readiness, security, academic and operations posture.
2. Security — MFA enrollment, recovery codes, secure authentication and chain verification.
3. Question Bank — banks, questions, independent review, publishing, blueprints and item analytics.
4. Grades & Credentials — grade schemes, transcript evidence and signed credentials.
5. Communications & Reports — templates, queued delivery, report generation and evidence.
6. Operations & Privacy — health snapshots, incidents, retention, privacy requests and encrypted backups.

The page uses the existing BioIntel visual identity, cards, status rails, protected forms and human-readable evidence panels. Raw technical details remain expandable rather than dominating normal user workflows.
