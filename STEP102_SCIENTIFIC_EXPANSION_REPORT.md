## Common operating model

Every workbench includes:

- deterministic demonstration data for immediate offline testing;
- CSV, TSV or JSON real-data import;
- strict required-column validation and safe failure messages;
- a five-stage workflow covering data, analysis, results, report/export and usage guidance;
- module-specific scoring, grouped summaries, quality or risk flags and visualisation;
- CSV result exports and JSON evidence manifests;
- explicit scientific, clinical, manufacturing, regulatory or privacy boundaries.

## Scientific boundary

These workbenches improve software breadth and exploratory research capability. They do not create genuine clinical validation, regulatory approval, manufacturing qualification, experimental confirmation or privacy certification. Publication, patient-care, GxP, production manufacturing and external data-sharing decisions still require authentic evidence and independent review.
