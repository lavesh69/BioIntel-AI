# Education Phase 8 App Experience

Phase 8 gives each major application a recognizable visual language:

- Connect: collaboration network and trust graph.
- Shiro AI: evidence orb, grounding pipeline and citation workspace.
- Studio: DNA and scientific operations cockpit.
- Classroom: schedule grid and academic operations palette.
- Science Pulse: scientific signal waveform and research trend identity.
- Academy: cinematic course gallery and learning-media identity.
- Universe: connected module constellation.
- Examinations: secure grid, monitoring and moderation identity.
- Intelligence: adaptive-learning and neural evidence identity.
- Institution: campus topology and operations identity.

The default sidebar is widened to prevent the truncated labels shown in the reported screenshots. Long application names wrap naturally instead of being clipped.
