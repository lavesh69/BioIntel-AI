## Export and traceability

- Every specialist output creates a manifest with table-level SHA-256 hashes.
- Specialist engine contracts are included in each module reproducibility ZIP.
- Actual specialist workspace output is included when generated during the session.
- Deterministic seed, simulation count and bounded execution contract are recorded.
- Starter or synthetic values are explicitly labelled and are not presented as real evidence.

## Stability and optimization

- All Monte Carlo engines have enforced iteration limits.
- Interactive row, task, site, risk and specimen limits protect memory.
- Invalid probabilities, negative capacities, duplicate identifiers and impossible counts are blocked before calculation.
- Literature duplicates are excluded from pooled estimates while the retained master remains reviewable.
- Task cycles and unknown dependencies fail safely.
- Individual specialist panels fail independently and preserve the rest of the module.
