# Step 89 — Independent Validation Accelerator Report

## Why the authentic rating cannot be forced to 10/10

The real external-validation rating is an evidence score, not a software-feature score. A legitimate 10/10 requires authentic program-specific proof across nine domains: independent clinical reports from multiple organizations, prospective multisite endpoint success, independent pharmaceutical laboratory replication, peer-reviewed publication, completed GxP operating history, regulatory inspection history, applicable authorization, approved patient-care use and post-market evidence.

Blank templates, public reference datasets, internal tests, generated reports, synthetic fixtures and self-attestation are deliberately excluded. Changing the displayed number without the corresponding third-party evidence would make the application scientifically misleading.

## Step 89 improvements

1. **Action-plan engine:** Every failed control is converted to a prioritized task with the responsible external owner, required artifact and acceptance evidence.
2. **Planning projection:** Users can model a route to 10/10 without modifying the actual score or release gate.
3. **Partner execution pack:** A checksum-manifested uncompressed ZIP can be sent to an independent clinical or pharmaceutical partner.
4. **Blinded exchange protocol:** Predictions are frozen and locked before adjudicated outcomes are released.
5. **Independent responsibilities matrix:** Sponsor, site, adjudicator, statistician and quality-review responsibilities are separated.
6. **Statistical analysis outline:** Primary endpoints, sample size, confidence intervals, site analyses, missing data, subgroups and sensitivity analyses are predefined.
7. **Evidence acceptance checklist:** Issuer independence, stable identifiers, file integrity, reviewer separation, intended-use match and fixture exclusion are machine-auditable.
8. **Budget-aware roadmap:** Retrospective feasibility, prospective pilot, powered multisite study, quality/regulatory pathway and post-market stages are separated.
9. **Final Assurance integration:** Accelerator readiness and authentic external evidence remain two visibly separate ratings.

## Expected practical effect

The software-side pathway is now 10/10 execution-ready. It reduces ambiguity, prevents missing documentation and makes it easier to engage an independent lab, university, hospital or CRO. Actual evidence scores will rise only after those organizations execute the work and their original controlled records pass independent review.
