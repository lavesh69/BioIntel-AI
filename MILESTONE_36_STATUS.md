## Evidence boundary

A repository-level 10/10 means the required software controls, tests, policies, deployment assets and documentation exist and pass the implementation matrix. It does **not** mean FDA approval, GxP certification, clinical validity, peer-review acceptance, prospective wet-lab validation or successful production inspection. Those outcomes require real data, operating history, independent validation and organization-specific quality approval.
