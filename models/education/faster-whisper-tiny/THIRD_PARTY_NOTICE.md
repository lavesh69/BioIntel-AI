This is the multilingual OpenAI Whisper tiny model converted to CTranslate2 format by SYSTRAN. BioIntel loads the bundled files with local_files_only enabled and CPU INT8 compute. Model output can contain recognition errors and must be reviewed by an authorized educator before publication or Shiro indexing.
