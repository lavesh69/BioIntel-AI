1. CNS Compound Optimization Board with configurable objective weights, reference deltas and Pareto-front identification.

6. Rare Neurological Disease Board combining variant triage with CNS target, disease, expression and model-organism context.
7. Neuro Multi-Omics Disease Signature Builder with multivariate cross-validated logistic signatures and coefficient summaries.
8. Digital Neurophysiology Review Board synchronizing channel QC, candidate events and reviewer decisions.

## Scientific boundary

All nine additions are research-screening and workflow-assistance capabilities. They do not establish clinical efficacy, safety, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, medical-device performance or controlled scientific release eligibility. External datasets, specialist engines, independent cohorts, prospective validation and responsible expert sign-off remain required.
