# BioIntel AI Milestone 29 Status

**Version:** 3.0.0  
**Milestone:** Biotechnology & Bioinformatics Core  
**Status:** Implemented and regression tested

## Working local modules

- FASTQ QC/trimming and assembly metrics
- VCF and GFF/GTF processing
- primer, CRISPR and codon-design screening
- transcriptomics normalization, differential screening and PCA
- protein annotation and structural PDB analysis
- ADMET/drug-likeness and pathway/network analysis
- qPCR, enzyme kinetics, growth and bioprocess calculations
- microbiome diversity, ANI proxy and multi-omics integration
- aligned-sequence consensus and UPGMA phylogeny
- safe optional external-tool adapters and capability detection

## Scientific boundary

Built-in datasets are demonstrations. Rule-based annotations and statistical
screens are research-support outputs, not clinical, diagnostic or publication-
grade replacements for validated tools such as DESeq2, InterPro, DeepTMHMM,
FastANI, MAFFT, BLAST+, HMMER, GATK or wet-lab validation.
