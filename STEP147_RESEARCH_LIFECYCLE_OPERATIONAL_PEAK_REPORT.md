# Step 147 — Research Lifecycle Operational Peak

Step 147 converts the four Step 146 lifecycle modules from readiness-oriented planning workspaces into operational research-lifecycle systems while preserving all 24 Research Planning modules and the Step 145 application-stability layer.

## Modules upgraded

1. Research Ethics, Consent & Participant Protection Studio
2. Research Data Management & FAIR Planning Studio
3. Data Monitoring, Interim Review & Safety Studio
4. Publication, Dissemination & Knowledge Translation Studio

## Ethics, consent and participant protection

- Consent version and language registry
- Participant-level consent option matrix
- Re-consent scheduling and urgency
- Ethics submission package readiness
- Committee query deadlines and escalation
- Participant-facing readability analysis
- Privacy-sensitive data-flow mapping
- Withdrawal and specimen/data-use impact analysis
- Cross-module impacts into Protocol, CRF and FAIR planning

## FAIR data management

- Metadata completeness and schema validation
- SHA-256/checksum verification tracking
- File-format and data-dictionary validation
- Provenance completeness
- Repository deposit readiness
- Access governance and consent restrictions
- Licence/access compatibility checks
- Twelve-month storage and replicated-capacity forecast
- Backup restore-test age and recovery readiness
- Retention, legal hold and destruction calendar

## Interim monitoring and safety

- Exposure-adjusted incidence rates
- Person-time and follow-up validation
- Time-to-first-event planning data
- Recurrent-event burden
- System-organ-class and preferred-term review
- Participant-level safety timeline
- Safety reporting deadlines and late-report escalation
- Site-level reporting signal detection
- Laboratory baseline-to-worst-grade shifts
- Interim efficacy/futility boundary review
- Conditional-power review
- Benefit-risk planning view

This remains a planning and monitoring aid and does not make medical or study-stopping decisions.

## Publication and dissemination

- Manuscript section production and word-count targets
- Claim-to-analysis-to-figure/table traceability
- Reporting guideline completion
- Authorship contribution, approval and conflict tracking
- Journal scope-fit and open-access cost planning
- Figure/table accessibility and source-data inventory
- Peer-review comment workflow
- Manuscript/preprint/submission version lineage
- Protocol-to-publication endpoint deviation detection
- Audience-to-channel dissemination mapping

## Visual depth

The four modules now expose 51 specialist visual pathways in addition to the shared Research Planning visual suite. These include consent lineage, privacy flow, storage forecasting, data-quality heatmaps, exposure-adjusted safety rates, interim boundaries, claim-evidence graphs, authorship matrices and protocol-to-publication deviation views.

## Operational exports

Every module exports all machine-readable result tables, reviews, hashes and one domain-specific operational document:

- Ethics Submission and Consent Operations Package
- Data Management and Repository Deposit Plan
- Interim Monitoring and Safety Review Package
- Manuscript and Dissemination Operations Package

## Stability and validation

- Backwards compatible with Step 146 minimum schemas
- Optional operational fields receive safe defaults
- Negative/impossible values blocked
- Duplicate identifiers blocked
- Bounded row and upload limits retained
- Deterministic calculations and SHA-256 manifests retained
- Panel-level recovery and stable error IDs retained
- Step 145 database, cache, job and startup hardening preserved

## External boundary

Automated tests can verify implementation behavior, data validation and package integrity. They cannot grant ethics approval, establish legal compliance, replace medical or monitoring-committee judgment, certify FAIR repository acceptance, or guarantee journal/publication outcomes.
