# Phase 14 Application Experience

A new **BioIntel Academic Quality & Intelligence Center** provides five desktop workspaces:

1. Overview — academic-quality posture, evidence coverage and qualification boundaries.
2. Outcomes & Evidence — outcome authoring, relationships, assessment mappings, evidence review, attainment and quality cycles.
3. Psychometrics — human-review item statistics, distractor behavior and reliability evidence.
4. Learner Support — transparent support snapshots and governed intervention cases.
5. Calendar & ICS — audience-aware institutional events, timetable visibility and portable calendar export.

The page uses the existing BioIntel visual system, role-aware actions, KPI cards, evidence-status badges, styled tables, actionable empty states and clear warnings against automatic high-stakes decisions or unsupported accreditation claims.
