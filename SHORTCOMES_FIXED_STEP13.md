# Step 13 — Professional Operations Shortcomings Fixed

## No multi-project governance

The workspace handled datasets and models but lacked a professional project
portfolio. Step 13 adds project codes, objectives, ownership labels, priorities,
statuses, target dates and evidence linkage.

## No executive-level summary

Workspace status was detailed but operationally scattered. Executive Overview
adds maturity scoring, risk counts, readiness components and priority projects.

## Reactive issue discovery

Users had to inspect multiple screens to find missing evidence or overdue work.
Activity & Risk Center now generates local actionable alerts automatically.

## Fragmented activity history

Project changes, experiments and audit events lived separately. Step 13 combines
them into one privacy-safe timeline.

## Consumer-style visual tone

Step 13 adds Executive and Clinical palettes, board KPIs, professional status
chips and calmer enterprise hierarchy.

## Reports lacked portfolio context

Research Report Studio now includes project portfolio and executive maturity
evidence.
