# Phase 12 Application Experience

The new **BioIntel Final Completion & Qualification Center** provides four desktop workspaces:

1. Overview — local code-completion posture and external qualification boundary.
2. Identity & Infrastructure — OIDC PKCE, PostgreSQL migration export/validation/apply and S3/MinIO readiness.
3. Academic Assurance — originality evidence, independent examination approvals, moderation, appeals and live candidate heartbeats.
4. Shiro & Operations — AI model profiles, hardware posture, RAG document safety and delivery providers.

The page uses the retained BioIntel visual system, role-aware navigation, KPI cards, empty states, governed forms and evidence tables. External provider actions remain disabled or fail honestly until administrators supply environment-backed credentials and reachable services.
