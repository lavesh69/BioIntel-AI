# Budget-aware staged validation roadmap

## Stage 1 — retrospective independent feasibility
Use an external institution's locked dataset and independent ground truth. This can improve scientific confidence,
but it does not replace a prospective clinical study or regulatory authorization.

## Stage 2 — two-site prospective pilot
Run a small preregistered study with locked predictions and adjudicated outcomes to identify operational failures.

## Stage 3 — adequately powered multisite validation
Execute the predefined primary endpoint across independent sites with independent statistical review.

## Stage 4 — quality-system and regulatory pathway
Operate change control, CAPA, training and post-deployment monitoring under the applicable quality system and
pursue jurisdiction-specific authorization when the intended use requires it.

## Stage 5 — post-market evidence
Collect authorized real-use performance, safety, complaint, drift and periodic-review evidence.
