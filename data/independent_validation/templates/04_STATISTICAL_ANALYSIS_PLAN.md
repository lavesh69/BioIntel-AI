# Statistical analysis plan — controlled outline

- Intended use and target population
- Primary and secondary endpoints
- Predefined success thresholds and confidence intervals
- Sample-size justification
- Missing-data and exclusion rules
- Site-level and pooled analysis
- Calibration, discrimination and decision-threshold analysis where applicable

- Sensitivity analyses

- Protocol deviations and analysis-set definitions

Complete this plan before prediction outcomes are unblinded.
