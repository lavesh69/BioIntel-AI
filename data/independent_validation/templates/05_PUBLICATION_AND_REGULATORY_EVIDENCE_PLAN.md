# Publication and regulatory evidence plan

- Register the study and retain the official identifier where applicable.
- Predefine authorship, data access and conflict-of-interest disclosures.
- Report negative, inconclusive and positive outcomes.
- Preserve methods, limitations, software/model version and statistical code.
- Obtain a DOI or PubMed identifier for a peer-reviewed publication when published.
- Maintain a jurisdiction-specific regulatory applicability assessment.
- Do not claim authorization until an active regulator-issued record matches the exact intended use.
