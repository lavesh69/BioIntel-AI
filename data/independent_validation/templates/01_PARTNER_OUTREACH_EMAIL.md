# Independent validation partner outreach

Subject: Independent validation collaboration request for BioIntel AI

We are seeking an independent clinical or pharmaceutical research partner to evaluate the documented intended use
of BioIntel AI under a preregistered protocol. The partner must retain scientific independence, control ground-truth
outcomes, receive locked predictions before outcome disclosure, and issue its own final report.

Requested capabilities:
- qualified investigators and documented training;
- ethics/regulatory determination where applicable;
- controlled source data and adjudicated outcomes;
- independent statistical review;
- permission to retain the final report, stable identifier and provenance metadata in the evidence registry.

This request does not ask the partner to endorse the product in advance.
