## Step 146 research lifecycle templates

- `research_ethics_consent_template.csv` — participant protection, consent, privacy and vulnerable-population safeguards.
- `research_data_fair_planning_template.csv` — identifiers, metadata, access, retention, repository, licence and backup planning.
- `research_interim_monitoring_safety_template.csv` — interim event monitoring, arm denominators, seriousness and review triggers.
- `research_publication_dissemination_template.csv` — publication, lay summary, data/code statement and accessibility planning.

These templates are local planning aids. They do not grant ethics approval, certify legal compliance, replace a monitoring committee or guarantee publication acceptance.

## Step 147 operational lifecycle columns

The four lifecycle templates now include optional operational fields while retaining the Step 146 minimum schemas:

- Ethics and consent: consent versions, participant choices, re-consent, submission/query deadlines, privacy flow and withdrawal impact.
- FAIR data management: checksums, schema validation, storage growth, repository deposition, access governance, restore tests and retention actions.
- Interim monitoring and safety: person-time, coded safety terms, reporting deadlines, laboratory shifts, interim boundaries and conditional power.
- Publication and dissemination: manuscript sections, claim-evidence links, reporting guidelines, authorship, journal selection, peer review and version lineage.

Blank optional fields are handled safely. Invalid ranges, impossible schema counts and negative exposure/storage values are blocked before analysis.
