# BioIntel Literature Notes

## Background
PTGS2 is associated with inflammatory pathways. ADORA2A is a G protein-coupled receptor and is often discussed with caffeine and adenosine.

## Demonstration findings
- Aspirin reference: PTGS2 IC50 125 nM
- Caffeine reference: ADORA2A Ki 820 nM
- Adenosine reference: ADORA2A Ki 32 nM
- Quercetin reference: PIK3CA IC50 950 nM

## Warning
These are illustrative values for software demonstration only.
