# NCBI GEO GSE2034 compact expression excerpt

Source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE2034

The full public series contains 286 human breast-cancer samples measured on GPL96. This bundled excerpt contains two probe rows and the first twenty sample columns exactly as displayed by the official GEO accession viewer. It is intended for format testing, software demonstrations and reproducibility checks only; it is not sufficient for biological inference or clinical decisions.
