# Step 141 — Research Planning Peak Suite

## Scope

Research Planning expanded from **2 modules to 18 modules**. The two existing workspaces remain preserved and sixteen new planning modules were added.

## New modules

- **Study Protocol Designer** — Build a complete, reviewable research protocol from objectives through visits, endpoints, deviations and amendments.
- **Sample Size & Statistical Power Studio** — Calculate transparent sample-size scenarios across continuous, binary, survival, repeated-measures and clustered designs.
- **Clinical Trial Design Studio** — Design phase-aware clinical trials with arms, cohorts, interim decisions, endpoints, recruitment and safety monitoring.
- **Experimental Design & DoE Workbench** — Create and analyse factorial, response-surface, screening and optimal experimental designs for laboratory and process studies.
- **Randomization, Blinding & Allocation Studio** — Generate reproducible allocation schedules with strata, variable blocks, concealment and balance diagnostics.
- **Cohort Definition & Eligibility Builder** — Define reproducible eligibility rules, estimate feasible cohorts and explain every inclusion or exclusion decision.
- **Endpoint & Outcome Strategy Studio** — Prioritise primary, secondary and exploratory endpoints with burden, validity, missingness and multiplicity controls.
- **Biomarker Study Planning Studio** — Plan diagnostic, prognostic and predictive biomarker studies from discovery through independent validation.
- **Sampling & Biospecimen Planning Studio** — Plan collection, aliquoting, storage, shipping and retention for biospecimen-intensive studies.
- **Data Collection & CRF Designer** — Design structured case-report forms, data dictionaries and validation rules across visits and study domains.
- **Statistical Analysis Plan Studio** — Build a versioned statistical analysis plan linking populations, endpoints, models, covariates, multiplicity and sensitivity analyses.
- **Research Feasibility & Recruitment Planner** — Forecast enrolment, screen failures, site activation, dropout, cost and study-completion probability.
- **Research Timeline, Milestones & Dependencies Studio** — Plan tasks, dependencies, critical paths, resources and risk-adjusted timelines across a research programme.
- **Research Risk & Mitigation Planner** — Identify, score, mitigate and monitor scientific, statistical, assay, data and operational research risks.
- **Evidence Gap & Literature Planning Studio** — Map existing evidence, contradictions and unanswered questions to justify and prioritise new research.
- **Pre-Registration & Reproducibility Studio** — Lock hypotheses, outcomes, exclusions, sample-size rationale and analysis decisions before observing study outcomes.

## Honest boundary

The suite provides research-planning calculations, documentation and decision support. It does not manufacture ethical approval, regulatory acceptance, clinical adjudication, statistical sign-off or real site feasibility evidence. Live Streamlit browser automation and a physical clean-Windows installation were not available in the Linux build environment.
