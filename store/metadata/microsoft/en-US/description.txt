BioIntel AI for Windows is a local-first Scientific Intelligence for Life Sciences that packages the application backend, reference data, models and professional interface into a desktop application.

Biology, Engineered into Intelligence.

Analyse biological sequences, molecular and assay data, genomics, transcriptomics, single-cell matrices, metagenomics, scientific documents and evidence-aware machine-learning workflows. Review data provenance, model limitations, connected-provider history, quality gates and reproducible reports from one workspace.

The Windows edition launches a private backend on the local computer and stores the working workspace under the current Windows user profile. Internet-connected providers are optional and explicitly invoked. No advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking are included.

BioIntel AI is for research and education. It is not a medical device, diagnostic system, treatment recommendation engine, regulatory approval or substitute for qualified scientific review.
