BioIntel AI is a connected Scientific Intelligence for Life Sciences for biotechnology, bioinformatics, research analytics and evidence-aware machine learning.

Biology, Engineered into Intelligence.

Use the iPhone or iPad app to securely connect to the BioIntel server operated by your organisation. The workspace provides guided tools for sequences, genomics, transcriptomics, single-cell analysis, metagenomics, protein interactions, cancer genomics, molecular research, scientific documents, provider lookup and model/evidence review.

Native connection features include network awareness, validated HTTPS workspace addresses, deep-link setup, sharing, haptic status feedback, privacy information and support access.

BioIntel AI contains no advertising SDKs and does not perform cross-app tracking. The mobile shell stores the chosen workspace address locally. Scientific data is handled by the server selected by the operator and is sent to external scientific providers only when an operator explicitly invokes a connected feature.

BioIntel AI is intended for research and education. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment advice, laboratory certification or regulatory approval. Scientific outputs require qualified review and independent validation before clinical, safety-critical, publication or commercial use.

A compatible HTTPS BioIntel server and authorised access may be required.
