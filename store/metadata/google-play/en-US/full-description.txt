BioIntel AI is a Scientific Intelligence for Life Sciences for researchers, students, data scientists and laboratory teams.

Biology, Engineered into Intelligence.

Connect the mobile app to a BioIntel server operated by your organisation and access guided scientific workflows across:

• DNA, RNA and protein sequence analysis
• FASTQ quality control and variant workbenches
• Transcriptomics, single-cell and metagenomics analysis
• Molecular descriptors, ADMET and DTI research workflows
• Protein interaction and comparative pathogen genomics projects
• Cancer genomics prioritisation and evidence-aware reporting
• Scientific provider lookup across approved public resources
• Document intelligence, knowledge search and reproducible exports
• Model quality, evidence, provenance and release-readiness review

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
The mobile app does not include advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. It stores only the selected workspace address on the device. Scientific data is processed by the BioIntel server selected and controlled by the operator. External providers are invoked only through explicit connected features.

RESEARCH-USE BOUNDARY
BioIntel AI supports research and education. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, regulatory approval or laboratory certification. Results require qualified review and independent validation before safety-critical, clinical, publication or commercial use.

A compatible HTTPS BioIntel server and authorised access may be required. Availability of connected scientific providers depends on the operator's configuration, provider terms and network access.
